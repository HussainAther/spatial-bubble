# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and constructive environment for
contributors, users, researchers, educators, and students. Scientific
disagreement should be handled carefully and professionally.

## Expected behavior

Positive behavior includes giving specific evidence-based feedback, stating
assumptions and uncertainty, respecting differences in experience, accepting
corrections, focusing criticism on ideas and methods, and helping others
reproduce results.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate
misrepresentation, publishing private information, repeated disruption,
fabricated evidence, and retaliation against good-faith reports are not
acceptable.

## Scientific disagreement

Disagreement about models, methods, evidence, or interpretation is welcome.
Contributors should identify the claim, applicable physical regime, units,
assumptions, reproducible evidence, and relevant references where possible.
Strong criticism of a method is acceptable; personal hostility is not.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions that violate this policy
and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Private reports
should be used when public discussion would expose private information or create
additional harm.

## Scope

This policy applies to repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions,
documentation, and project-related public communications.
