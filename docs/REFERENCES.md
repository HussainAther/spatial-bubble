# Initial references

This is a seed bibliography, not yet a systematic review.

1. Young, T. (1805), *An Essay on the Cohesion of Fluids*.
2. Laplace, P.-S., *Mécanique céleste*, supplement to Book X.
3. Orfanidis, S. J., *Electromagnetic Waves and Antennas*, multilayer
   structures (freely available textbook).
4. Katsidis, C. C. and Siapkas, D. I. (2002), "General transfer-matrix method
   for optical multilayer systems with coherent, partially coherent, and
   incoherent interference," *Applied Optics* 41(19), 3978--3987.
5. Vaynblat, D., Lister, J. R. and Witelski, T. P. (2001), "Rupture of thin
   viscous films by van der Waals forces: evolution and self-similarity,"
   *Physics of Fluids* 13, 1130.
6. Careaga, J. et al. (2024), "Application of the thin-film equations in
   modelling of Marangoni flow patterns amongst surfactant source and drain
   locations," arXiv:2407.11868.
7. Matsumura, K. and Shiono, M. (2014), "Interference Experiment of a
   Soap-Bubble Film by Optical Spectroscopy," *Journal of Science Education in
   Japan* 38(3), 188--195, doi:10.14935/jssej.38.188.

Each implemented model must later cite the exact derivation and validation data
it uses; a general bibliography is not sufficient provenance.
